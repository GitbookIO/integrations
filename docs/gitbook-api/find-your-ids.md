# Find your IDs

To use the GitBook API, you need your organization, space, and site IDs. All three appear directly in the GitBook app URL.

## **Organization and space IDs**

When you're in a space, look at the URL in your browser. It follows this pattern:

```
https://app.gitbook.com/o/<organization_id>/s/<space_id>
```

For example, in `https://app.gitbook.com/o/50mEth1Ng/s/RaNd0Mstr1NG`:

* The value after `/o/` — `50mEth1Ng` — is your organization ID.
* The value after `/s/` — `RaNd0Mstr1NG` — is your space ID.

## **Site ID**

Open the site in GitBook and look at the URL. It contains `/sites/<site_id>`.

For example, in a URL containing `/sites/site_w0r1d`, `site_w0r1d` is your site ID.
